# Data Privacy Notice

Last updated: 17 October 2026

## Who is responsible

Luthgarda Pazon is the personal information controller for personal data you submit on this website. She is Senior Sales at Alveo Land. She is not Alveo Land Corp., and this site is not the official Alveo Land website.

Email her at [luthgarda@citadel-codex.com](mailto:luthgarda@citadel-codex.com).

This notice describes the inquiry form. The first-visit acknowledgement of the Terms, the Privacy Policy, and this notice is stored only in your browser. It is not sent to her, and it is not consent for an inquiry.

## What the inquiry form collects

You decide whether to send an inquiry. If you do, the site stores:

- your name
- your email, your mobile number, or both
- the type of inquiry
- the property, if you select one
- how you prefer to be reached
- for a viewing, whether you want an on-site visit or a video call, and a preferred date if you give one
- where you are based
- whether you live or work outside the Philippines
- the best time to reach you
- your message
- the fact that you ticked the consent box, and the date and time of that tick

The form includes a hidden field named for automated checks. It is not a question for you. If that field contains anything, the submission is treated as automated and is not saved.

The form does not ask for a government ID, a payment card, or a password. This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies, and it does not take payment.

## Why she uses it

She uses these details to read your inquiry and reply, including about a project, a viewing, or payment terms you asked about. She uses your preferred channel, your location, whether you are abroad, and the best time to reach you so she can reply in a practical way.

## Legal basis

The legal basis is your consent. The inquiry is saved only if you tick the box agreeing that the details in the form may be used to reply to that inquiry. The time of that consent is stored with the inquiry.

If you do not tick the box, the form is not accepted. You may still email her.

She relies on legitimate interest only to discard automated submissions that fill the hidden field, because that keeps the inquiry channel usable for real visitors, and those submissions are not stored.

## How long she keeps an inquiry

She keeps an inquiry until she has finished the conversation with you, and no longer than two years after the last message in that conversation.

If you started a transaction and she still needs the inquiry for it — for example, a reservation you asked her to pass along — she may keep the details that transaction still needs until that need ends. She then deletes them.

## Who she shares it with

Only she reads inquiries, through the site admin. She does not sell personal data.

The hosting provider that stores the database processes the inquiry so the site can save it and show it to her.

She gives details to Alveo Land only if you ask her to pass a reservation along, and only the details needed for that request.

## Servers outside the Philippines

The database may sit on a server outside the Philippines. If it does, your inquiry is stored and processed in that country so she can receive it and reply. The host’s role is limited to storing and serving that database for this site.

## Security

Inquiries are kept in the site database and are not shown on the public pages. Opening them requires her admin login. Published pages are meant to use an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Email her if you sent an inquiry by mistake or believe someone else has seen it.

## Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules, you have the right:

- to be informed of the processing described in this notice
- to access the inquiry she holds about you
- to correct details that are wrong
- to object to processing
- to ask her to erase or block the inquiry
- to data portability, meaning a copy you can take elsewhere
- to complain, and to damages where the law provides them

Email [luthgarda@citadel-codex.com](mailto:luthgarda@citadel-codex.com) to exercise any of these rights. Include your name, the email or mobile number you used on the form, and what you want her to do. She will reply within a reasonable time. She may ask for enough information to confirm she is dealing with the right person.

## Complaints to the National Privacy Commission

You may lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at [privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph). This notice is not an approval, registration, or opinion of the Commission.

## Children

This site is not for minors. Do not submit an inquiry for anyone under 18. If a minor’s details were sent, email her and she will delete them.

## Payments

An inquiry is not a payment. Do not send money to her personal account. Payments for a property go only to Alveo Land’s official accounts.
