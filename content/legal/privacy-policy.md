# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

## Who controls personal data on this site

Luthgarda Pazon is the personal information controller for personal data collected through this website. She is Senior Sales at Alveo Land. This is her personal site. It is not the official website of Alveo Land Corp., and Alveo Land Corp. does not control inquiries sent here.

You can reach her only at [luthgarda@citadel-codex.com](mailto:luthgarda@citadel-codex.com).

## What you can do here without an account

The public pages show Alveo project names, cities, statuses, and prices marked “to be confirmed,” plus her name, two photographs, and her email. You can read those pages without creating an account.

This site does not use analytics, does not set advertising cookies, and does not take payments.

## The first-visit acknowledgement

The first time you visit, the site asks you to acknowledge the Terms, this Privacy Policy, and the Data Privacy Notice before you use it. Each document opens on its own page, so you can read them before you accept.

That acknowledgement is stored only in your browser (local storage). It is not sent to her server, and she does not keep a copy of it.

## Inquiries

If you choose to send the inquiry form, the details you enter are saved in the site database so she can read them and reply. The fields, the purpose, the consent checkbox, and how long she keeps an inquiry are set out in the [Data Privacy Notice](/data-privacy).

The inquiry checkbox is separate from the first-visit acknowledgement. Ticking it applies only to that inquiry.

The form has a hidden field meant to catch automated submissions. Leave it blank. If it is filled in, the submission is not saved.

A separate one-hour hash can limit repeated submits, and Cloudflare Turnstile may later receive a token to check a submit. Both are described in the [Data Privacy Notice](/data-privacy). Neither is written on the inquiry. Turnstile is off while its keys are unset.

## Legal basis

She uses the details in an inquiry because you consent, by ticking the inquiry checkbox, to their use in replying to that inquiry.

She relies on legitimate interest only to keep the public pages available, to drop automated form fills that are not real inquiries, and to keep a one-hour hash that limits repeated submissions, because that protects the channel visitors use to write to her and does not build a profile of people who only browse.

## Who else handles the data

She reads inquiries herself, in a private admin area. She does not sell personal data.

The hosting provider that stores the database processes inquiry records so the site can save them and show them to her. If that server is outside the Philippines, those records are stored there for that purpose.

She shares details with Alveo Land only if you ask her to pass a reservation along, and only the details that request needs.

## Security

Inquiry contents are not published on the public pages. Reading them requires her admin login. When this site is published, pages are meant to be served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Email her if you think an inquiry was exposed or sent by mistake.

## Children

This site is not for minors. She does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor sent an inquiry, email her and she will delete it.

## Payments

This site does not process payments. Money for a property is paid only through Alveo Land’s official payment channels. Do not send payment to her personal account.

## Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules, you may be informed, access your data, ask for correction, object, ask for erasure or blocking, ask for data portability, and complain. The [Data Privacy Notice](/data-privacy) explains how to exercise those rights by emailing her, and how to complain to the National Privacy Commission.
